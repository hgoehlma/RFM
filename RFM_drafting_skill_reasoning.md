`v1.0.0` // `module_reasoning` // [living]

---

## The Problem

Content written into an RFM document goes wrong while it is being written, because the writer holds the reasoning and produces text at the same time.

The writer was in the conversation and the later reader was not. What the writer takes for granted is what that reader lacks: a term from the conversation, a reason given as who decided it or when it was found, a sentence that leans on another document, content put where it happened to come up, a statement that the next planned step will make untrue.

Catching any of this means stepping out of writing. A later sweep catches some of it, but by then the draft exists, and a paragraph that exists is harder to question than one that does not.

## The Assumptions

**[AS-LOAD] - The writer does not step out of writing to check unless something prompts it at that moment.** Checking where content belongs, or whether a sentence says how things are, means stopping production to look at it. This breaks if writers reliably check on their own while drafting.

**[AS-FAMILIAR] - Knowing a problem well is easily taken for knowing what already exists.** A Landscape drafted from familiarity with the problem looks complete. It breaks if writers reliably raise the need for research themselves.

## The Landscape

What helps a writer get text right at the moment of writing?

| Approach | What it does | Why it's insufficient |
|---|---|---|
| A style guide | States conventions in a document of its own | It is read when someone goes to it, which is rarely while writing. |
| A prose linter | Flags listed words and patterns | It catches only what it lists. Most of these failures have no fixed pattern. |
| A writer's checklist | Questions run before submitting | Close to what is needed. Reported item by item, it becomes a record of having checked rather than a better draft. |

**The gap:** none of these sits inside the act of writing and checks the draft against the failures that occur there without turning the check into a report.

## The Options Considered

**Separate skills for before drafting, after drafting and after a Landscape.** Rejected because the three moments fall inside one act, often one turn: three skills would each depend on the same weak trigger, and three descriptions of one act sound alike, which lowers the chance the right one loads.

**Moving the skill into the always-on text.** Rejected because the skill holds formats and tests that a turn without drafting does not need, and the always-on text holds only what is acted on wherever it is read.

**Printing the check in the reply, one question at a time.** It would show that the check ran. Rejected because it turns the reply into an audit of the draft, and the human reads the record of checking instead of the draft.

## The Chosen Direction and Why

**Why the skill gives the writer the reader's view**

The writer cannot stop being the one who was there. So the skill brings the later reader into the act of writing at the two points where changing course is still cheap: before the first sentence, to decide where that reader will look for the content, and after the draft, before it is shown, to read it as someone who was not in the conversation.

A new term is where this matters most. The writer coins it knowing what it means, and the reader meets it without that. A term the glossary already holds for the same thing is the better word, and spares the reader one thing under two names. A word the set already uses for something else misleads them. So before a term is used throughout, the glossary is read for the thing and the set is searched for the word.

A sentence about what another document says or does is checked against that document's text before it stays. The writer remembers a document as it was meant, and a paraphrase from memory can drop a condition the text holds, leaving a claim the document does not make.

**Why the skill loads while writing**

Every part of the skill is needed while content is being written, or just after, before it is shown or written to file. Writing is the act it loads on. The trigger is weak, because the writer has to recognize that drafting is about to start, and a miss leaves text that looks like ordinary work. So the description names the moment in the words used at it, and the always-on text tells the writer to load the skill before drafting for any project document.

**Why the check shows in the draft, not in the reply**

The check exists to improve the draft, so its result is the draft after the fixes. Where a fix changes what we shaped together, the reply says so in a sentence, because the draft then differs from what both of us built up. A check that did not run leaves the failures in the draft, where reading it shows them.

**Why a revision shows the old text beside the new**

Text already in a document is text both of us agreed to. A revision shown on its own lets us see what is new but not what went. Beside the old text, the change mostly explains itself, so a reason is added only where it does not.

**Why research comes before a Landscape**

A Landscape drafted from familiarity looks complete, so before a Landscape is drafted, the LLM says whether it thinks research should come first, and why. Afterwards the work goes back to the Problem and the Assumptions, because research often sharpens the gap or shows it is already filled. If it is filled, the LLM proposes stopping the document there, and we agree on it, because the human may see a part of the gap the research does not reach.

Where the research finds no settled words for the problem, each new term the work is using is treated as a glossary candidate before it hardens. While the problem is still taking shape, a word coined in passing gets used, starts to sound settled, and steers the thinking before anyone has chosen it.

## The Boundaries

**Not language patterns.** Em dashes, inflated importance and AI vocabulary belong to the unslop skill.

**Not whole-document reads.** The same argument made twice, or content ready to graduate, is found by the sweeps.

**Not RFM's own documents in particular.** The skill goes to every project run with RFM and holds nothing about RFM's own set.

## The Open Questions

**[OQ-LOAD] Does the always-on instruction to load the skill reliably produce the load?**

The trigger is weak and a miss is silent. If the load is often missed, the skill needs a second way in.

**[OQ-RAN] Without the check printed, does a missed check show?**

The draft carries the evidence, but only for failures a reader notices while reading it. If drafts keep reaching a document with failures the check should have caught, the check needs a visible trace that is not a report.

## The Hard Lessons

**[HL-TEMPLATE] With an entry nearby to copy, the LLM drafts without the skill.**

When the document already holds an entry of the kind being written, the LLM takes its shape from that entry, and the work stops reading to it as drafting. The skill does not load, and the draft follows its neighbour's form instead of what its section is for. A neighbour named as the reason for a shape is the sign, and either of us can hear it.
