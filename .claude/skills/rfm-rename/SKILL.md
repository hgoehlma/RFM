# Rename

Run this for each change. A search for an earlier change does not cover a new one.

## Before the change: search

Search for:
- the old name
- its file name or named ID, if it has one
- its head word alone ("mode" from "execution mode"), matched as a whole word
- the other words the set uses for the same subject

Search the reasoning documents, the operational documents and everything derived from them, across the whole project. Leave out logs and the handover note.

Open each hit and read its passage. Decide whether the passage only uses the word, or depends on what it meant.

## Say the scope

Before making the change, tell me in plain sentences, document by document:
- what you will change: passages that only use the word or the status
- what needs thinking through together: passages whose argument rested on the old meaning or status, and any entry the change leaves with nothing live
- which related words you searched for, so I can add one you missed

No patterns, raw output or counts: they turn the scope into a report of the search. If nothing elsewhere refers to it, say so in a sentence.

## Carry it out

- Make the change and fix the first group without asking about each one. Where a redefinition changed what a passage says, rewrite the passage, not only the word.
- Bring the second group to me, with what you would do with each. Don't cut or rewrite reasoning on your own. Whether an emptied entry goes, shrinks or moves is decided with the curation skill.
